Dropo's Android bridge links Xray-core in-process to support VLESS XHTTP
subscriptions. A scoped MPL-2.0 lifecycle modification isolates HTTP pools
and cancels XHTTP requests when their VPN session ends. Complete corresponding
modified source files are included beside this notice under xray-core-source/:
client.go.txt, dialer.go.txt, managed_session.go.txt and managed_session_test.go.txt.
The pinned upstream source linked above supplies the remaining component files.
The source modification is applied by a verified Go build overlay; upstream
module-cache files are not changed. No separate Xray executable, shell
runtime, first-run executable download, or upstream database asset is used
by this bridge. The Dropo integration is maintained separately in the
app/mobile/dropoandroid and app/mobile/dropocore directories.
